# P0.25b.1 — Independent paper cash history replay

Goal: reconstruct synthetic cash balances and monthly transfers from complete evidence, without trusting the current RiskState balances. This is a research accounting audit, not financial execution.

Design: keep legacy PaperBook transitions unchanged; add a separate versioned evidence envelope and strict audit function. The envelope records the initial month-start opening balance, then explicit month-close records with boundary timestamp, previous month, carry, net, simulated external top-up, withdrawal, and protected balance. The existing RESERVE/SETTLE/PROFIT_LOCK entries are retained as distinct match journal events. Rebuild using the calendar-ordered stream, processing close records before any match event at the exact boundary. Require a start in January at Bangkok midnight with 1000 synthetic units (no omitted earlier year-loss history). Require one close per elapsed month; reject skipped/duplicate/misdated closes, malformed/nonfinite cash, missing reserve side/odds, mismatched end state, missing/extra profit transfers, and invalid future entries. No automatic migration or inferred historical odds/funding.

Cash semantics: at close, net = free + protected + open cost basis - 1000. topup=max(-net,0), withdrawal=max(net,0). These are explicitly hypothetical simulated transfers, never verified actual payments. Reset free=1000-open cost basis and protected=0. Annual negative month sum resets on new year. Record month-start funding in opening evidence, and cumulative net withdrawals/topups separately; never count topups as profit. Compare all balance and period fields, including open position attributes, from replay. Use strict Decimal, with no rounding beyond existing simulation convention.

The audit optionally compares an independently supplied SHA256 of canonical normalized complete evidence (opening, journal and close records), while never claiming external authenticity just from a supplied hash. The digest has a version tag and deterministic UTC timestamps/decimal representations. No changes to selection or market policy. All data synthetic; no allocation or live operations.

API: paper_cash_history.py: OpeningEvidence, MonthCloseEvidence, cash_history_sha256(book, opening, closes), audit_cash_history(book, opening, closes, as_of, *, expected_sha256=None), close_evidence_from_report(report, boundary). Return aggregate balances/transfer counts, internal cash consistency and anchor match, external authenticity false. Missing historical evidence must fail closed. This stage is a strict verifier and helper, not a persisted append-only store; external archival and CLI/producer integration remain separate.

Acceptance: test hand-calculated opening, loss and win across boundaries, capital conservation, forged state, missing/duplicate close, changed payout, invalid profit lock, missing expected lock, future time, altered hash, legacy missing prices; full pytest and 4-job CI before review.
